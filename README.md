# Bank Risk Assessment Register 🏦🔐

## Objective
This project is a professional risk register created as part of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate. The goal is to evaluate potential security risks for a commercial bank, calculate risk severity, and prioritize remediation efforts.

## Scenario
I acted as a cybersecurity analyst for a commercial bank located in a coastal area. The bank employs 120 staff members and manages 2,200 accounts. The organization must comply with strict financial regulations while partnering with local businesses for marketing. 

## Methodology
The assessment uses a standard risk matrix formula: 
**Risk Priority = Likelihood (1-3) × Severity (1-3)**

Risks evaluated include:
* Business Email Compromise (BEC)
* Compromised User Databases (Poor Encryption)
* Financial Records Leak (Exposed Backups)
* Physical Theft
* Supply Chain Disruption (Natural Disasters)

## Key Findings
* **Highest Priority:** Exposed database servers pose the most critical and immediate threat due to the ease of automated discovery by malicious actors.
* **Moderate Priority:** Social engineering (BEC) and weak encryption require significant mitigation strategies, including employee training and cryptographic upgrades.
